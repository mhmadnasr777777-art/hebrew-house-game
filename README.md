# 🏠 לעבת הבית - Hebrew House Game

## Description | תיאור

An interactive 3D house exploration game entirely in Hebrew! Walk through different rooms (living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom) and interact with various objects. The game features:

- **3D Graphics** using Three.js
- **WASD Movement Controls** - just like your favorite games!
- **Mouse Camera Control** - look around freely
- **Hebrew Text-to-Speech** - all interactions and room names spoken in Hebrew
- **Interactive Objects** - click on furniture and items to hear descriptions

## How to Play | איך משחקים

1. **Start the Game** - Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. **Move Around** - Use WASD keys to walk through the house
3. **Look Around** - Move your mouse to look in different directions
4. **Interact** - Click on objects to hear Hebrew descriptions
5. **Explore** - Visit all rooms and interact with everything!

## Technical Stack | סטק טכנולוגי

- **Three.js** - 3D Graphics Engine
- **Web Audio API** - Sound Effects
- **SpeechSynthesis API** - Text-to-Speech (Hebrew)
- **Vanilla JavaScript** - No frameworks needed
- **WebGL** - GPU-accelerated 3D rendering
